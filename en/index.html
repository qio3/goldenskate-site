The Russian school

Beautiful skating starts with the basics.

We are the first and only Russian-school figure skating club in Samsun. Before jumps come edges, posture and coordination — not quick results, but technique that lasts.

All three in one club, on one membership.

On ice

Gliding, edges, spins and jumps. Every skater’s mistakes are worked through one by one.

Ballet & choreography

Posture, flexibility and musicality. A programme is more than elements — it is the beauty of movement.

Off-ice

Strength, power and coordination. Every jump is learned in the gym first, then taken to the ice.

Founder

GoldenSkate is the Samsun branch of the “Golden Star” school network from the Moscow region. The network has more than 15 coaches; the best of them are in Samsun.
